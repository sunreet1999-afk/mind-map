How your notes become a map
  • Paste a paragraph: its main word becomes the centre, sentences on the same topic are grouped, long sentences split into parts
  • First short line + a blank line becomes the centre (or a # Heading)
  • ## Headings become branches
  • A line ending in :, or followed by bullets, holds the lines under it
  • Indenting nests deeper
  • Topic: a, b, c makes three children
  • #tag anywhere groups those lines together
  • A blank line ends the current group
